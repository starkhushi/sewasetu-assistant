Three ways to apply

📱

On this website

Six short steps on any phone. Takes about 10 minutes.

💬

With an AI assistant

Ask ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant to apply for you. It asks one question at a time. How it works

🏛

At the block office

The counter or a CSC kiosk will fill it in with you.

Whichever way you apply, it is the same application with the same rules, and it shows up in the same place.

How to apply

  1. Verify the applicant's mobile number with an OTP.
  2. Fill in personal details, address and bank details.
  3. Upload one age-proof document.
  4. Read the preview, accept the declaration and note down the Application Number.

Keep these ready

  • The applicant's own mobile phone (for the OTP)
  • Birth certificate, school leaving certificate or Voter ID (a photo is fine)
  • Bank passbook: account number and IFSC, in the applicant's own name

Aadhaar is not needed.

After you apply

The block officer decides within 15 days. If no decision is made in that time, the law approves the application automatically. The pension is paid from the month you applied.

For help, visit your nearest Block Development Office.
