Apply with an AI assistant

You can ask an AI assistant to fill the pension form for you. You just talk to it, the way you would talk to someone at the counter.

Easiest: chat right here No app to install, nothing to set up. Open the chat assistant

Or use an AI assistant you already have, if it supports connectors:

How it works

  1. 1Add Sewa Setu to your assistant Once only. In your assistant's settings, add a connector and paste this link: {{ citizen_url }}
  2. 2Sign in with your mobile number This website opens. Type the applicant's mobile number and the OTP, then press Allow. This is how the portal knows whose application it is. The assistant never sees the OTP, and the sign-in lasts 2 hours.
  3. 3Just talk Say what you need, in your own words. The assistant asks one question at a time and reads everything back before sending.

On this demo portal the OTP is not sent to a real phone; read it in the SMS inbox.

What it looks like

Apply for old age pension for my grandmother. She is 70.
Sure. What is her full name, exactly as written on her age proof (Voter ID, birth or school certificate)?
Parboti Gogoi
Thank you. What is her date of birth as written on that card?
04/03/1956
… Here is everything I will send. Name: Parboti Gogoi. Date of birth: 04/03/1956. Village: Lakhipur, Sonari block. Bank account ending 8901, SBI. Is all of this correct?
Yes, submit.
Done. Application number SSP26482913. An SMS has gone to her phone. The officer decides within 15 days; if not, it is approved automatically.

It can help you

  • Apply for the pension
  • Tell you where your application is, in plain words
  • Explain why the money has not come, and record a complaint
  • Do the yearly "I am alive" confirmation

It cannot

  • Approve a pension. Only the officer can.
  • Skip any rule of the form (age 60+, one application per person)
  • See your OTP or act after your sign-in ends (2 hours)

An application made through an assistant is the same as one made on this website or at the counter. You can see it under My application, and end the assistant's sign-in any time from Connected applications.

For block officers

Officers have their own assistant: it shows what is waiting, opens a case, and records approve or reject in your name. Add this link instead, and sign in with your departmental username and password (no OTP):

{{ officer_url }}

Then ask, for example: "What is waiting for me?", "Show me the oldest case", "Approve it", or "Reject it, age proof not legible".

For technical people

The assistants connect through the Model Context Protocol (MCP) over streamable HTTP. Sign-in is OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, issued by this portal ({{ issuer }}). The citizen and officer servers are separate; a citizen's assistant never sees the officer's tools.

Citizen MCP endpoint{{ citizen_url }}
Officer MCP endpoint{{ officer_url }}

In any assistant that supports MCP: add the endpoint as a remote MCP server (streamable HTTP), then sign in when it asks; this portal's sign-in page opens in the browser. How I tested this: evaluation dashboard.
