Apply with an AI assistant

Any AI assistant that speaks the Model Context Protocol can apply for the pension and follow it up for a citizen, once that citizen signs in. Block officers have a separate endpoint with their own tools. Both follow the same rules as the form on this site: an assistant cannot get anyone a pension the counter would not.

How signing in works

  1. The assistant connects and is sent to this portal's sign-in page.
  2. Citizens enter their mobile and the OTP; officers use the departmental login.
  3. You see what the assistant will be allowed to do, and press Allow.
  4. The assistant acts for you for 2 hours (officers: one working day). It never sees the OTP or password.

You can end a sign-in early from Connected applications. Sign-in uses OAuth 2.1 with PKCE, issued by {{ issuer }}.

Connect from Claude Code

claude mcp add --transport http sewasetu-citizen {{ citizen_url }}
claude mcp add --transport http sewasetu-officer {{ officer_url }}

Then type /mcp in Claude Code, choose the server and Authenticate; this portal's sign-in page opens in the browser.

What the citizen's assistant can do

  • Explain the scheme and who is eligible.
  • Fill the application one question at a time, attach the age proof through a one-time link opened on the phone, read everything back, and submit only after the declaration.
  • Explain the status in plain words: who decided and why, which bank account the money goes to, the month the pension runs from, and when the yearly life certificate is due.
  • Withdraw a pending application, raise a complaint when the money has not come, confirm the yearly life certificate.

What the officer's assistant can do

  • Show what is waiting, oldest first, with the days left before automatic approval.
  • Open one case with its audit trail, the points worth a second look, and the document.
  • Approve, or reject with a reason, recorded in the officer's own name.
  • Show and close citizens' complaints.

Applications made through an assistant are marked via assistant on the officer's pages and in the audit trail. See how I tested this on the evaluation dashboard.
