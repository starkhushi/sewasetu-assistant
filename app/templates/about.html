About the Scheme

About this website This is a demonstration project built for a fellowship assignment (Build for Bharat). The state of Purvanchal, its Department of Social Welfare and this pension scheme are fictional, and every record in the database is invented. It shows how AI assistants could help people apply for a welfare scheme through an official, rule-following channel. No real government service is offered here, and no SMS is sent to any real phone.

The Old Age Pension Scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal, under the State Social Assistance Programme.

Eligibility: residents of Purvanchal aged 60 years and above. One application per eligible citizen. Applications are processed within 15 days as per the Purvanchal Right to Public Services Act; applications not processed within the statutory period stand approved (deemed approval).

Pension amount: Rs. 250 per month, credited by DBT to the applicant's bank account.

Required at application: a mobile number (for OTP), bank account and IFSC, and one age proof document (birth certificate, school leaving certificate or Voter ID; JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB).

One active application per applicant. A pending application can be withdrawn from the status portal and a fresh one submitted.
